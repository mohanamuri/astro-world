# The 12 Houses — Life Areas (Vedic + Western)

## House 1 — Self, Body, Personality (Lagna / Ascendant)
The First House is the most personal house — it represents your physical body, appearance, overall personality, and how the world first sees you. The sign rising on the eastern horizon at birth (your Ascendant or Lagna) sets the tone for your entire chart.

**Life areas:** Physical appearance, health, first impressions, self-image, early childhood.
**Vedic:** Lagna — the foundation of the entire kundali. Determines house positions for all other planets.

---

## House 2 — Wealth, Speech, Family (Dhana Bhava)
The Second House governs personal finances, accumulated wealth, possessions, voice and speech, and immediate family. It also relates to self-worth and what you value.

**Life areas:** Money, savings, possessions, food, speech, family of origin, early childhood values.

---

## House 3 — Communication, Siblings, Courage (Parakrama Bhava)
The Third House covers short-distance travel, communication, siblings, and mental courage. It governs writing, learning, neighbors, and everyday interactions.

**Life areas:** Brothers and sisters, writing, speaking, short journeys, social media, local community.

---

## House 4 — Home, Mother, Emotional Foundation (Sukha Bhava)
The Fourth House represents your home, emotional security, mother, and inner life. It shows the environment you grew up in and what gives you a sense of belonging.

**Life areas:** Home, real estate, mother, emotional security, childhood, private life, vehicles.

---

## House 5 — Creativity, Children, Romance (Putra Bhava)
The Fifth House governs creative expression, children, romance, and intelligence. It is the house of joy, fun, gambling, speculation, and artistic talent.

**Life areas:** Children, love affairs, creativity, arts, sports, speculation, intelligence, past-life merit.

---

## House 6 — Health, Service, Enemies (Shatru Bhava)
The Sixth House covers daily work routines, health, service to others, and enemies or obstacles. Strong planets here help overcome obstacles; weak planets may create health or conflict issues.

**Life areas:** Daily work, health, illness, debts, enemies, pets, service, competition.

---

## House 7 — Partnerships, Marriage (Kalatra Bhava)
The Seventh House is the primary house of marriage and partnerships. It represents your spouse or business partner and the nature of your one-on-one relationships.

**Life areas:** Marriage, romantic partnerships, business partnerships, contracts, open enemies, public life.

---

## House 8 — Transformation, Secrets, Inheritance (Ayu Bhava)
The Eighth House governs deep transformation, death and rebirth, sexuality, occult knowledge, inheritance, and other people's resources. It rules hidden things and sudden changes.

**Life areas:** Death, inheritance, shared finances, sexuality, occult, transformation, crisis, longevity.

---

## House 9 — Philosophy, Fortune, Father (Dharma Bhava)
The Ninth House is the house of higher wisdom, spirituality, long-distance travel, and good fortune. It represents dharma (life purpose), teachers, father, and religious beliefs.

**Life areas:** Higher education, religion, philosophy, long journeys, father, teachers, luck, publishing.

---

## House 10 — Career, Status, Authority (Karma Bhava)
The Tenth House is the most visible house — it represents career, public reputation, status, and authority. It shows what you achieve in the world and how you are seen professionally.

**Life areas:** Career, profession, public image, government, authority figures, ambition, social status.

---

## House 11 — Gains, Friends, Aspirations (Labha Bhava)
The Eleventh House governs income from profession, social networks, friends, groups, and long-term goals. It is the house of abundance and fulfillment of desires.

**Life areas:** Income, gains, friends, social groups, aspirations, elder siblings, networking.

---

## House 12 — Liberation, Loss, Foreign Lands (Vyaya Bhava)
The Twelfth House represents hidden realms — spirituality, isolation, losses, foreign countries, hospitals, and liberation (moksha). It governs what we give up or let go of.

**Life areas:** Isolation, foreign lands, spiritual liberation, hidden enemies, sleep, expenses, spiritual practice.

---
