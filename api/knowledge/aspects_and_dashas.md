# Planetary Aspects — Western Astrology

Aspects are angular relationships between planets that show how their energies interact.

## Conjunction (0° ± 8°)
Two planets in the same zodiac position. Their energies merge and intensify each other. Can be very powerful — either very positive or very challenging depending on the planets involved.
**Effect:** Amplification, fusion, intensity, focus.
**Examples:** Sun conjunct Jupiter = expanded confidence and luck. Mars conjunct Saturn = frustrated energy, disciplined action.

## Sextile (60° ± 6°)
A harmonious 60-degree angle. Planets work together easily and create opportunities. Less automatic than a trine — you need to take action to use it.
**Effect:** Opportunity, talent, cooperation, flow.
**Examples:** Venus sextile Jupiter = social charm and good fortune in relationships.

## Square (90° ± 7°)
A challenging 90-degree angle. Creates tension and friction between two planetary energies. The most motivating aspect — discomfort that forces growth and action.
**Effect:** Tension, challenge, motivation, conflict, growth.
**Examples:** Sun square Saturn = tension between self-expression and limitation, leading to maturity.

## Trine (120° ± 8°)
The most harmonious aspect. Planets in trine flow naturally together with ease and talent. The gift aspect — but can lead to laziness because things come too easily.
**Effect:** Ease, natural talent, harmony, flow, blessing.
**Examples:** Moon trine Venus = natural charm, emotional warmth, artistic ability.

## Opposition (180° ± 8°)
Two planets directly opposite each other. Creates polarity and tension that requires balance and integration. Often involves relationships and projecting qualities onto others.
**Effect:** Polarization, projection, balancing act, awareness through contrast.
**Examples:** Sun opposite Moon = tension between ego and emotions, public vs private self.

# Vimshottari Dasha — Planetary Period Meanings

## Ketu Dasha (7 years)
Period of detachment, spiritual growth, and past karma resolution. Material losses often occur, but spiritual gains are significant. Good for meditation and inner work. Sudden changes, isolation, and unexpected events.

## Venus Dasha (20 years)
Longest dasha — a golden period of love, beauty, wealth, and pleasure. Relationships flourish, creative talents emerge, and material comfort increases. Best period for marriage, arts, and luxury.

## Sun Dasha (6 years)
Period of self-expression, authority, and recognition. Career advancement and government connections. Good for leadership roles. Father figures become important. Health of eyes and heart needs attention.

## Moon Dasha (10 years)
Emotionally rich period. Mental peace, relationships with mother and women, fluctuations in mood and fortune. Good for business with the public. Travel over water. Intuition heightened.

## Mars Dasha (7 years)
Active, energetic, and sometimes aggressive period. Good for taking bold actions, physical activities, and property matters. Conflicts and accidents possible. Brothers play important roles.

## Rahu Dasha (18 years)
Period of ambition, material worldly success, and sometimes confusion. Unconventional paths bring results. Foreign travel and connections. Can bring sudden rise or fall. Illusions and obsessions.

## Jupiter Dasha (16 years)
Most auspicious dasha — wisdom, expansion, children, marriage, spiritual growth, and good fortune. Teaching, learning, and religious activities are favored. Financial prosperity and social recognition.

## Saturn Dasha (19 years)
Period of hard work, discipline, delays, and ultimate reward. Karmic debts are paid. Success comes slowly but surely. Old structures are tested. Service, responsibility, and persistence are key.

## Mercury Dasha (17 years)
Period of communication, intellect, business, and learning. Good for studies, writing, trading, and technology. Multiple activities and interests. Analytical thinking and logical problem-solving.
