# Zodiac Signs — Traits, Elements, Modalities

## Aries (Ram) — Vedic: Mesha
**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Mars
**Keywords:** Bold, pioneering, impulsive, energetic, competitive
Aries is the first sign — the initiator. Aries energy is raw, direct, and courageous. They lead with action before thinking. Natural entrepreneurs and athletes. Can be impatient and self-centered.

## Taurus (Bull) — Vedic: Vrishabha
**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Venus
**Keywords:** Patient, sensual, stubborn, reliable, pleasure-seeking
Taurus values stability, beauty, and comfort. They build slowly and last long. Natural artists and farmers. Deeply connected to the physical world. Can be possessive and resistant to change.

## Gemini (Twins) — Vedic: Mithuna
**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Mercury
**Keywords:** Curious, communicative, versatile, witty, restless
Gemini is the eternal student — always curious about everything. Masters of communication and ideas. Can hold two opposing views simultaneously. Can be superficial or scattered.

## Cancer (Crab) — Vedic: Karka
**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Moon
**Keywords:** Nurturing, intuitive, emotional, protective, home-loving
Cancer is the mother of the zodiac. Deep emotional intelligence and strong intuition. Fiercely protective of loved ones. Can be moody and overly sensitive. Home is their sanctuary.

## Leo (Lion) — Vedic: Simha
**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Sun
**Keywords:** Confident, generous, dramatic, creative, warm-hearted
Leo is the natural performer and leader. Generous and warm, they light up every room. Need recognition and appreciation. Can be arrogant or attention-seeking when insecure.

## Virgo (Maiden) — Vedic: Kanya
**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Mercury
**Keywords:** Analytical, detail-oriented, helpful, perfectionist, practical
Virgo serves through precision and analysis. Natural healers and problem-solvers. Excellence in craft and service. Can be overly critical — of themselves most of all.

## Libra (Scales) — Vedic: Tula
**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Venus
**Keywords:** Diplomatic, fair, relationship-oriented, indecisive, artistic
Libra seeks balance and harmony in all things. Masters of diplomacy and aesthetics. See all sides of every issue. Can be indecisive and people-pleasing to a fault.

## Scorpio (Scorpion) — Vedic: Vrishchika
**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Mars (Vedic) / Pluto (Western)
**Keywords:** Intense, perceptive, transformative, secretive, powerful
Scorpio goes deep — in feelings, research, and transformation. Magnetic and intense. Fearless in facing what others avoid. Can be controlling or vengeful when hurt.

## Sagittarius (Archer) — Vedic: Dhanus
**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Jupiter
**Keywords:** Adventurous, philosophical, optimistic, freedom-loving, blunt
Sagittarius is the seeker of truth and wisdom. Loves travel, philosophy, and expanding horizons. Naturally optimistic and inspiring. Can be tactless or commitment-averse.

## Capricorn (Sea-Goat) — Vedic: Makara
**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Saturn
**Keywords:** Ambitious, disciplined, pragmatic, patient, responsible
Capricorn climbs mountains — steadily, persistently. Master builders of structures and careers. Respect authority and tradition. Can be cold, overly serious, or workaholic.

## Aquarius (Water-Bearer) — Vedic: Kumbha
**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Saturn (Vedic) / Uranus (Western)
**Keywords:** Innovative, humanitarian, independent, eccentric, progressive
Aquarius is ahead of their time. Visionary thinkers committed to humanity. Deeply independent and unconventional. Can be emotionally detached or rebellious for its own sake.

## Pisces (Fish) — Vedic: Meena
**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Jupiter (Vedic) / Neptune (Western)
**Keywords:** Compassionate, intuitive, dreamy, spiritual, selfless
Pisces is the last sign — the completion of the zodiac cycle. Deep compassion and spiritual sensitivity. Natural empaths and artists. Can lose themselves in others or in escapism.
