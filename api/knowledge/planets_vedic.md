# Vedic Planets (Navagrahas) — Meanings and Significations

## Sun (Surya)
The Sun represents the soul, self, identity, vitality, and life force. It governs authority, leadership, father, government, and career. A strong Sun gives confidence, willpower, and recognition. A weak or afflicted Sun can cause ego issues, poor health, and conflicts with authority.

**In houses:**
- Sun in 1st house: Strong personality, leadership qualities, may be self-centered.
- Sun in 2nd house: Focus on family wealth, speech; may be authoritative in family.
- Sun in 4th house: Happiness from home, may have issues with mother or domestic peace.
- Sun in 7th house: Dominating partner, delays in marriage, powerful relationships.
- Sun in 10th house: Excellent for career and fame; natural authority and public recognition.

**Exalted in Aries, Debilitated in Libra.**

---

## Moon (Chandra)
The Moon represents the mind, emotions, intuition, mother, and nurturing. It governs mental peace, imagination, adaptability, and public life. A strong Moon gives emotional intelligence and empathy. A weak Moon leads to anxiety, mood swings, and mental instability.

**In houses:**
- Moon in 1st house: Emotional, intuitive personality; fluctuating health and moods.
- Moon in 4th house: Deep connection to home and mother; emotionally secure.
- Moon in 7th house: Emotional partnerships; partner may be nurturing and sensitive.
- Moon in 10th house: Public recognition; career in caring professions.

**Exalted in Taurus, Debilitated in Scorpio.**

---

## Mercury (Budha)
Mercury represents intellect, communication, analytical ability, commerce, and siblings. It governs writing, speaking, business, mathematics, and technology. A strong Mercury gives sharp intellect and communication skills. A weak Mercury causes nervousness and communication problems.

**In houses:**
- Mercury in 1st house: Intellectual, witty, and communicative personality.
- Mercury in 3rd house: Excellent writer or speaker; strong short journeys and siblings.
- Mercury in 6th house: Analytical problem-solver; good at service industries.
- Mercury in 10th house: Career in communication, media, business, or technology.

**Exalted in Virgo, Debilitated in Pisces.**

---

## Venus (Shukra)
Venus represents love, beauty, luxury, relationships, creativity, and arts. It governs romance, marriage, material pleasures, and artistic expression. A strong Venus brings charm, wealth, and harmonious relationships. A weak Venus causes relationship problems and lack of aesthetic sense.

**In houses:**
- Venus in 1st house: Charming, attractive, and artistic personality.
- Venus in 5th house: Creative talent, romantic nature, enjoyment of life.
- Venus in 7th house: Beautiful and loving partner; harmonious marriage.
- Venus in 10th house: Career in arts, beauty, entertainment, or luxury goods.

**Exalted in Pisces, Debilitated in Virgo.**

---

## Mars (Mangal / Kuja)
Mars represents energy, courage, action, ambition, and conflict. It governs physical strength, competition, brothers, land, and engineering. A strong Mars gives determination and physical prowess. A weak Mars causes aggression, accidents, and impulsiveness.

**Note on Mangal Dosha:** Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 creates Mangal Dosha — tension in marriage. Both partners having Mangal Dosha cancels the effect.

**In houses:**
- Mars in 1st house: Energetic, courageous, aggressive personality.
- Mars in 3rd house: Brave, ambitious; good at competitive sports or military.
- Mars in 7th house: Passionate but dominating partner; Mangal Dosha.
- Mars in 10th house: Dynamic career; leadership in engineering, military, sports.

**Exalted in Capricorn, Debilitated in Cancer.**

---

## Jupiter (Guru / Brihaspati)
Jupiter represents wisdom, expansion, spirituality, good fortune, and abundance. It governs teachers, children, higher learning, religion, and philosophy. A strong Jupiter brings blessings, prosperity, and spiritual growth. A weak Jupiter causes poor judgment and lack of faith.

**In houses:**
- Jupiter in 1st house: Generous, wise, spiritually inclined personality.
- Jupiter in 5th house: Blessed with children; intelligence and creative talent.
- Jupiter in 9th house: Deep spirituality, philosophical wisdom, fortune.
- Jupiter in 11th house: Enormous gains and fulfillment of desires.

**Exalted in Cancer, Debilitated in Capricorn.**

---

## Saturn (Shani)
Saturn represents discipline, karma, hard work, delays, and transformation. It governs limitations, responsibility, service, old age, and longevity. A strong Saturn gives perseverance and just rewards after hard work. A weak Saturn causes suffering, delays, and chronic problems.

**Sade Sati:** Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from natal Moon — a 7.5-year period of challenges and deep transformation.

**In houses:**
- Saturn in 1st house: Serious, disciplined, slow-moving but steady personality.
- Saturn in 3rd house: Persistent efforts; success through hard work in communication.
- Saturn in 7th house: Delayed marriage; serious and responsible partner.
- Saturn in 10th house: Slow but steady career growth; authority through hard work.

**Exalted in Libra, Debilitated in Aries.**

---

## Rahu (North Node)
Rahu represents obsession, ambition, material desires, foreign influences, and illusions. It is a shadow planet (no physical body) that amplifies the qualities of the sign and house it occupies. Rahu brings sudden changes, unconventional thinking, and worldly success.

**In houses:**
- Rahu in 1st house: Unusual personality; strong drive for recognition.
- Rahu in 7th house: Unconventional relationships; foreign or unusual partner.
- Rahu in 10th house: Ambitious career goals; success through unconventional means.
- Rahu in 11th house: Great material gains; large social network.

---

## Ketu (South Node)
Ketu represents spirituality, detachment, past karma, liberation, and mysticism. It is the opposite of Rahu — where Rahu desires, Ketu detaches. Ketu gives deep intuition, spiritual wisdom, and in some areas sudden losses leading to liberation.

**In houses:**
- Ketu in 1st house: Spiritual personality; may feel detached from physical world.
- Ketu in 4th house: Detachment from home and mother; spiritual home life.
- Ketu in 12th house: Strong spiritual liberation; interest in meditation.
