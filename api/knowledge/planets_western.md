# Western Planets — Psychological Meanings

## Sun
The Sun in Western astrology represents the core self, ego, identity, and life purpose. It shows where you shine, how you express yourself consciously, and what drives your sense of achievement. Your Sun sign is the sign the Sun was in on your birthday.

**Aries Sun:** Bold, pioneering, impulsive. Natural leader who acts first and thinks later.
**Taurus Sun:** Patient, sensual, determined. Values security, comfort, and beauty.
**Gemini Sun:** Curious, communicative, versatile. Needs constant mental stimulation.
**Cancer Sun:** Nurturing, emotional, protective. Home and family are central to identity.
**Leo Sun:** Confident, creative, generous. Needs recognition and loves to perform.
**Virgo Sun:** Analytical, detail-oriented, service-focused. Strives for perfection.
**Libra Sun:** Diplomatic, fair, relationship-oriented. Seeks harmony and balance.
**Scorpio Sun:** Intense, transformative, perceptive. Goes deep in everything they do.
**Sagittarius Sun:** Adventurous, philosophical, freedom-loving. Seeks truth and expansion.
**Capricorn Sun:** Ambitious, disciplined, pragmatic. Builds long-term structures.
**Aquarius Sun:** Innovative, humanitarian, independent. Ahead of their time.
**Pisces Sun:** Compassionate, intuitive, imaginative. Deeply empathetic and spiritual.

## Moon
The Moon represents the emotional self, subconscious, instincts, and what we need for security. It shows how you react emotionally, what comforts you, and your relationship with your mother and home. The Moon changes signs every 2.5 days.

**Aries Moon:** Emotional reactions are quick and direct. Needs action to feel secure.
**Taurus Moon:** Needs physical comfort, stability, and sensory pleasure for emotional security.
**Gemini Moon:** Emotional processing through talking and thinking. Needs variety.
**Cancer Moon:** Highly sensitive and nurturing. Home is the emotional center.
**Leo Moon:** Needs appreciation and creative expression to feel emotionally fulfilled.
**Virgo Moon:** Finds comfort in routines, helpfulness, and having things organized.
**Libra Moon:** Needs harmony in relationships; upset by conflict or injustice.
**Scorpio Moon:** Deep emotional intensity; needs trust and transformation.
**Sagittarius Moon:** Emotional freedom and adventure. Feels confined by too much routine.
**Capricorn Moon:** Emotional security through achievement and self-control.
**Aquarius Moon:** Detached emotionally; needs mental connection and freedom.
**Pisces Moon:** Deeply intuitive and empathetic; absorbs others' emotions.

---

## Mercury
Mercury represents communication, thinking style, learning, and information processing. It governs how you speak, write, reason, and connect with others mentally.

---

## Venus
Venus represents love style, aesthetic sense, values, and pleasure. It shows what attracts you, how you give and receive love, and what you find beautiful.

---

## Mars
Mars represents drive, ambition, sexuality, and assertiveness. It shows how you take action, what angers you, and how you pursue what you want.

---

## Jupiter
Jupiter represents expansion, opportunity, luck, and philosophy. It shows where you are naturally fortunate and how you grow and expand.

---

## Saturn
Saturn represents discipline, structure, limitations, and long-term achievement. It shows where you face challenges that ultimately build strength and mastery.

---

## Uranus
Uranus represents sudden change, innovation, rebellion, and awakening. It spends 7 years in each sign, affecting entire generations. It shows where you break from tradition.

---

## Neptune
Neptune represents dreams, illusions, spirituality, and transcendence. It dissolves boundaries and connects you to something larger than yourself.

---

## Pluto
Pluto represents transformation, power, death and rebirth, and deep psychological change. It shows where you undergo profound transformation over decades.
