# 27 Nakshatras — Lunar Mansions

Each nakshatra spans 13°20' of the zodiac. The Moon's position at birth determines your birth nakshatra (Janma Nakshatra), which is foundational to Vedic astrology.

---

## 1. Ashwini (0°–13°20' Aries)
**Symbol:** Horse's head | **Deity:** Ashwini Kumaras (divine healers) | **Lord:** Ketu
Quick, energetic, healing energy. People born under Ashwini are fast, independent, and pioneering. Natural healers and pioneers. Impulsive but with the ability to recover quickly. Good at starting new things.

## 2. Bharani (13°20'–26°40' Aries)
**Symbol:** Yoni (womb) | **Deity:** Yama (god of death/dharma) | **Lord:** Venus
Intense creative and destructive energy. Bharani natives are passionate, disciplined, and carry heavy burdens with resilience. Strong sense of dharma. Can be stubborn. Creative and fertile minds.

## 3. Krittika (26°40' Aries–10° Taurus)
**Symbol:** Razor/flame | **Deity:** Agni (fire god) | **Lord:** Sun
Sharp, purifying, and courageous. Krittika natives are determined, straightforward, and have the ability to cut through illusion. Nurturing but also capable of fierce criticism. Strong willpower.

## 4. Rohini (10°–23°20' Taurus)
**Symbol:** Chariot | **Deity:** Brahma | **Lord:** Moon
Most fertile and beautiful nakshatra. Rohini natives are charming, creative, and materially blessed. Strong attachment to sensory pleasures, beauty, and luxury. Highly artistic. May be possessive.

## 5. Mrigashira (23°20' Taurus–6°40' Gemini)
**Symbol:** Deer's head | **Deity:** Soma (Moon god) | **Lord:** Mars
Searching, curious, and gentle. Mrigashira natives are always searching for something — love, truth, knowledge. Sensitive, alert, and creative. Good at research and creative writing.

## 6. Ardra (6°40'–20° Gemini)
**Symbol:** Teardrop/diamond | **Deity:** Rudra (storm god) | **Lord:** Rahu
Stormy, transformative energy. Ardra natives go through intense emotional experiences that eventually lead to wisdom. Intellectual curiosity, strong analytical ability. Can be destructive before rebuilding.

## 7. Punarvasu (20° Gemini–3°20' Cancer)
**Symbol:** Quiver of arrows | **Deity:** Aditi (mother of gods) | **Lord:** Jupiter
Return, renewal, and abundance. Punarvasu brings the ability to recover from setbacks and start anew. Spiritual, philosophical, and generous. Good teachers and counselors. Optimistic nature.

## 8. Pushya (3°20'–16°40' Cancer)
**Symbol:** Flower/cow's udder | **Deity:** Brihaspati (Jupiter) | **Lord:** Saturn
Most auspicious nakshatra. Pushya natives are nurturing, reliable, and devoted. Strong sense of duty and care for others. Natural teachers, advisors, and healers. Patience and wisdom.

## 9. Ashlesha (16°40'–30° Cancer)
**Symbol:** Serpent | **Deity:** Nagas (serpent deities) | **Lord:** Mercury
Penetrating, mystical, and sharp. Ashlesha natives have keen perception and can see through deception. Hypnotic charm and intelligence. Can be manipulative. Strong healing and occult abilities.

## 10. Magha (0°–13°20' Leo)
**Symbol:** Royal throne | **Deity:** Pitrs (ancestors) | **Lord:** Ketu
Royal, proud, and ancestral. Magha natives have strong connections to their lineage and ancestors. Natural leaders with authority and presence. High self-respect and dignity. Achievement-oriented.

## 11. Purva Phalguni (13°20'–26°40' Leo)
**Symbol:** Hammock/front legs of bed | **Deity:** Bhaga (god of delight) | **Lord:** Venus
Pleasure, creativity, and relaxation. Purva Phalguni natives are charming, creative, and enjoy the finer things in life. Natural performers and artists. Romantic and sociable. Need balance between work and rest.

## 12. Uttara Phalguni (26°40' Leo–10° Virgo)
**Symbol:** Back legs of bed | **Deity:** Aryaman (patronage) | **Lord:** Sun
Service, commitment, and steady relationships. Uttara Phalguni brings lasting partnerships and steady achievement. Helpful, friendly, and reliable. Good at social service and creating stable foundations.

## 13. Hasta (10°–23°20' Virgo)
**Symbol:** Hand | **Deity:** Savitar (Sun god of skill) | **Lord:** Moon
Skillful, practical, and dexterous. Hasta natives are resourceful, witty, and good with their hands. Excellent craftspeople, healers, and service providers. Adaptable and clever.

## 14. Chitra (23°20' Virgo–6°40' Libra)
**Symbol:** Bright jewel/pearl | **Deity:** Vishwakarma (divine architect) | **Lord:** Mars
Brilliant, creative, and artistic. Chitra natives are drawn to beauty, design, and craftsmanship. Sharp minds with an eye for perfection. Architects, designers, and artists. May be vain.

## 15. Swati (6°40'–20° Libra)
**Symbol:** Sword/young shoot | **Deity:** Vayu (wind god) | **Lord:** Rahu
Independent, restless, and diplomatic. Swati natives value freedom above all. Flexible, charming, and business-minded. Good at trade, diplomacy, and adapting to new situations.

## 16. Vishakha (20° Libra–3°20' Scorpio)
**Symbol:** Triumphal arch | **Deity:** Indra and Agni | **Lord:** Jupiter
Purposeful, ambitious, and intense. Vishakha natives are focused on their goals and determined to achieve them. Strong sense of purpose. Can be aggressive in pursuit of ambitions.

## 17. Anuradha (3°20'–16°40' Scorpio)
**Symbol:** Lotus | **Deity:** Mitra (god of friendship) | **Lord:** Saturn
Devoted, disciplined, and cooperative. Anuradha natives form deep, loyal friendships. Strong organizational ability and spiritual depth. Good at working in groups toward a common goal.

## 18. Jyeshtha (16°40'–30° Scorpio)
**Symbol:** Circular amulet | **Deity:** Indra (king of gods) | **Lord:** Mercury
Senior, protective, and intense. Jyeshtha natives are the eldest — responsible, protective, and sometimes dominating. Sharp intelligence and strong presence. Natural leaders.

## 19. Mula (0°–13°20' Sagittarius)
**Symbol:** Bunch of roots | **Deity:** Nirriti (goddess of destruction) | **Lord:** Ketu
Deep roots, dissolution, and liberation. Mula natives go to the root of everything — philosophically inclined, research-oriented. May experience significant losses that lead to profound transformation.

## 20. Purva Ashadha (13°20'–26°40' Sagittarius)
**Symbol:** Fan/tusk | **Deity:** Apas (water goddess) | **Lord:** Venus
Invincible, persuasive, and purifying. Purva Ashadha natives are determined and can withstand great challenges. Philosophical and motivating. Good at influencing others.

## 21. Uttara Ashadha (26°40' Sagittarius–10° Capricorn)
**Symbol:** Tusk/plank | **Deity:** Vishwadevas | **Lord:** Sun
Steadfast, principled, and victorious. Uttara Ashadha natives achieve lasting victory through righteousness and perseverance. Responsible and respected leaders.

## 22. Shravana (10°–23°20' Capricorn)
**Symbol:** Ear/footsteps | **Deity:** Vishnu | **Lord:** Moon
Learning, listening, and connection. Shravana natives learn through listening and are excellent at connecting people and information. Knowledgeable, perceptive, and spiritually oriented.

## 23. Dhanishtha (23°20' Capricorn–6°40' Aquarius)
**Symbol:** Drum/flute | **Deity:** Ashta Vasus | **Lord:** Mars
Abundance, rhythm, and fame. Dhanishtha natives are ambitious, musical, and seek material abundance. Athletic ability and strong organizational skills. Can be overly materialistic.

## 24. Shatabhisha (6°40'–20° Aquarius)
**Symbol:** Empty circle/100 stars | **Deity:** Varuna (god of cosmic law) | **Lord:** Rahu
Healing, mystery, and solitude. Shatabhisha natives are healers, researchers, and seekers of hidden knowledge. Independent and secretive. Strong interest in medicine, technology, or the occult.

## 25. Purva Bhadrapada (20° Aquarius–3°20' Pisces)
**Symbol:** Funeral cot | **Deity:** Aja Ekapad (one-footed goat) | **Lord:** Jupiter
Transformative, intense, and idealistic. Purva Bhadrapada natives are passionate and willing to sacrifice for their ideals. Deep thinkers who can burn with a great inner fire.

## 26. Uttara Bhadrapada (3°20'–16°40' Pisces)
**Symbol:** Twins/back legs of funeral cot | **Deity:** Ahir Budhnya (serpent of the deep) | **Lord:** Saturn
Wise, deep, and compassionate. Uttara Bhadrapada natives are mature souls with great wisdom and inner depth. Excellent counselors and spiritual teachers. Patient and profound.

## 27. Revati (16°40'–30° Pisces)
**Symbol:** Fish/drum | **Deity:** Pushan (nourisher/guide) | **Lord:** Mercury
Nourishing, gentle, and abundant. Revati is the last nakshatra, completing the cycle. Natives are nurturing, artistic, and spiritually evolved. Natural guides who help others find their path.
